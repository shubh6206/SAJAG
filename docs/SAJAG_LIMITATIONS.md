# SAJAG System Limitations & Operational Boundaries
**Project**: SAJAG – AI-Powered Personnel Stress & Welfare Monitoring System  
**SIH Problem Statement**: SIH26186 (Ministry of Home Affairs)  

---

## 1. Data Fidelity & Research Grounding

### 1.1 Synthetic Demonstration Data
- Current demonstration datasets are **synthetically modeled** based on operational parameters of CAPF personnel deployed in Line of Actual Control (LAC), Counter-Insurgency (CI), and Border Outpost (BOP) duties.
- Synthetic datasets are constructed using known physiological ranges (resting heart rates, photoplethysmography [PPG] heart rate variability, actigraphy sleep staging) published in military ergonomics literature.

### 1.2 No Real Patient or Service Identifiers
- Names, force numbers, and medical notations in the demonstration environment are entirely synthetic placeholders generated solely for SIH hackathon evaluation.

---

## 2. Technical & Hardware Constraints

### 2.1 Photoplethysmography (PPG) in Extreme Climates
- High-altitude sub-zero environments (e.g. Siachen, Eastern Ladakh) induce peripheral vasoconstriction, which can degrade optical PPG signal-to-noise ratios.
- **Mitigation**: The SAJAG baseline engine detects signal degradation, flags `LOW CONFIDENCE`, and prompts manual verification rather than generating misleading risk estimates.

### 2.2 Tactical Intermittent Sync (Radio Silence)
- During tactical patrols or border outposts with operational radio silence, real-time wireless synchronization is disabled.
- **Mitigation**: SAJAG Edge architecture operates on asynchronous, store-and-forward batch ingestion via USB dock or local base station sync upon patrol return.

---

## 3. Organizational & Behavioral Boundaries

### 3.1 Voluntary Check-In Underreporting
- Due to military culture and perceived stigma around seeking assistance, personnel may initially underreport voluntary emotional or family concerns.
- **Mitigation**: SAJAG emphasizes objective operational load (consecutive night duties, overdue leave) so the system protects personnel even when they do not self-report.

### 3.2 Non-Clinical & Non-Punitive Boundary
- SAJAG is an operational welfare decision-support tool. It must **never** be used as evidence for disciplinary proceedings, demotions, or punitive personnel actions.
- Recommendations generated by the system are advisory; final decisions remain exclusively with human commanders, medical officers, and welfare counselors.
