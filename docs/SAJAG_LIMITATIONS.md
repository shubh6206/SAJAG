# SAJAG Operational & Scientific Limitations
**SIH26186 Prototype Release** | **Ministry of Home Affairs**

---

## 1. Non-Diagnostic Boundaries
- **No Psychiatric Diagnosis**: SAJAG does not diagnose clinical depression, PTSD, bipolar disorder, anxiety disorders, or acute suicidal ideation.
- **Decision-Support Only**: All scores and tiers are prototype risk indicators intended to guide human welfare officers in scheduling voluntary check-ins and duty rest.
- **No Disciplinary Automation**: SAJAG cannot and must not be used to automate performance penalties, disciplinary inquiries, or punitive duty assignments.

---

## 2. Sensor & Biometric Limitations
- **Wearable Sensor Accuracy**: Consumer photoplethysmography (PPG) sensors may experience motion artifacts during high-intensity tactical movements.
- **Baseline Calibration Window**: A minimum of 7 to 14 days of historical data is recommended for optimal statistical baseline confidence.
- **Environmental Factors**: Extreme heat, cold, or high-altitude hypoxia can alter resting physiological vitals independent of psychological stress.

---

## 3. Dataset & Field Readiness
- **Academic Benchmark Scope**: WESAD and SWELL-KW are laboratory datasets and do not represent active combat or tactical border deployments.
- **Field Validation Requirement**: Prior to operational deployment across CAPF battalions or police stations, empirical field validation under the supervision of qualified medical and welfare authorities is mandatory.
